Costs more than it looks

Read before you sit down

Crimson Room. Cocktails are a fair 320 to 380 at the bar, but tables carry an 8,000 weekday and 10,000 weekend minimum. Sit at the bar unless you have a group.

Tichuca. Adds 17% to cocktails already at 400 to 600, and turns you away without a physical passport.

Mae Khlong Hua Pla Mo Fai. Looks like a fish shack, bills like a restaurant. 800 to 1,200 a head.

Dog in Town. Flat 350 entry, one drink included, stay as long as you like.

Book, or turn up early

  • Book well ahead: MIA, Saawaan, Pizza Studio Tamaki.
  • No Name Noodle takes bookings when the line opens at 09:00 and sells out within minutes. Thirty-five bowls a day.
  • Gone by lunchtime: Urai Braised Goose, Je Ngor, Ten Suns. Treat their closing times as optimistic.

Corrections from your original list

  • CDRGE is CDGRE, a Gaggan Anand and Sühring brothers cafe. There is no Cédric Grolet in Bangkok. Their old domain now redirects to an unrelated site, but the venue is open.
  • Patina has permanently closed. It was on your cafe list; the Talat Noi shophouse is gone. Hong Sieng Kong nearby covers the same territory.
  • The Cassette Music Bar in Ekkamai has closed. Listed here is the pink Siam Square coffee bar instead, which fits your "just for Instagram" note better anyway.
  • Saawaan lost its Michelin star in 2023 and has not regained it. iODE was never starred.
  • Zaew is a Bib Gourmand, not a starred restaurant, whatever the aggregators say. It is also a fishball and yentafo shop rather than boat noodles.
  • Peace Oriental's Ekkamai branch is gone, redeveloped into a condo. Sukhumvit 49 and Amarin are the live ones.
  • Iron Balls is two venues, and neither is on Soi 49: the distillery at Ekkamai, the parlour at Soi 45.
  • Larder is on Sukhumvit 39 and shuts at 17:00. A daytime deli, not a dinner plan.
  • Namsu is mid-move from Chinatown to above Rangoon Tea House. Check Instagram before travelling.
  • Also: Ten Sons → Ten Suns, Siwilai Music Club → Sound Club, Mae Klong Huo Pia Mor Fai → Mae Khlong Hua Pla Mo Fai, Khua Kling Pad Sod → Pak Sod.
